Event Activities

Five powerful sessions designed to spark ideas and build connections

01

Ask Me Anything Cards

Every participant gets a small card to write one question for the founder. Anonymous, honest, and unfiltered.

02

Founder Failure Moment

"Tell us about one decision that failed—and what you learned from it."

This usually creates a much more authentic conversation.

03

Startup Challenge

Participants get 15–20 minutes to identify a problem and create a basic solution/business model.

04

60-Second Pitch

Each selected team gets exactly one minute to pitch:

• Problem  • Solution  • Target customer  • Why it can work

05

Networking Corner

During coffee, participants introduce themselves using:

Name + Background + Business/Interest + What I'm Looking For
